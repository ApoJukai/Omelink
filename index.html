Questions, answered

Good to know.

OmeLink is intentionally simple. Here is what to expect before beginning a conversation.

Do I need an account?+

No. OmeLink is designed to start without registration, profile creation, or a social feed.

Are chat messages stored?+

OmeLink does not save chat messages in its application database. Firebase is used temporarily for matchmaking between available peers.

Can I use OmeLink without a camera?+

Yes. Choose Text Only to chat without camera or microphone access. Video Chat requires browser permission for both.

What happens if no interest match is available?+

After a short interest-based search, OmeLink can continue by looking for an available random match.

Can I end a conversation at any time?+

Yes. You can skip to another person, pause matchmaking, or leave the current conversation from the app controls.
